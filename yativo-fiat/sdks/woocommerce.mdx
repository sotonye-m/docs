Yativo Payments adds two payment methods to your WooCommerce checkout:

- **Local payments:** bank transfers and local methods for your country. Your customer pays on a secure Yativo page.
- **Cards:** debit and credit cards, Apple Pay and Google Pay on Yativo's secure page, for USD stores. Card details never touch your site.

An order is only marked paid after the plugin confirms the payment with the Yativo API. It works with the classic and block checkout, and with WooCommerce order storage (HPOS).

## Set up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install">
    In WordPress, go to **Plugins → Add New → Upload Plugin**, choose `yativo-payments.zip`, then click **Install Now** and **Activate**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your Yativo account">
    Go to **WooCommerce → Settings → Yativo**. Choose **Sandbox (test)**, paste your [API key and secret](/yativo-fiat/api-keys), and click **Save changes**. Then click **Test connection**.

    If it says the IP address isn't allowed, add your web server's IP to the key's [IP whitelist](/yativo-fiat/ip-allowlist).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on webhooks">
    Click **Register webhook URL**. The plugin registers `https://your-site.com/wp-json/yativo/v1/webhook` and saves the secret for you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your payment methods">
    In **WooCommerce → Settings → Payments**, open **Yativo local payments**, tick **Enable**, enter your customers' three-letter country code (such as `BRA` or `MEX`), click **Load payment methods** and tick the ones you want.

    For cards, open **Yativo cards**, tick **Enable** and enter the **seller customer ID**: the Yativo customer whose `card_checkout` endorsement is approved.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make a test purchase">
    Buy something in a private browser window. For cards in sandbox, use `4242 4242 4242 4242` with any future expiry and any CVC. The order moves to **Processing** once Yativo confirms the payment.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  A Yativo account has one webhook URL. **Register webhook URL** replaces any URL set before. If another system uses your Yativo webhooks, register that system's URL and forward events to the store instead.
</Warning>

When everything works, switch to **Production**, enter your production key and secret, and click **Register webhook URL** again.

## What happens to orders

| Yativo says | The order becomes |
|---|---|
| Payment confirmed and the amount matches | **Processing** (paid) |
| Payment confirmed but the amount is different | **On hold**, with a note |
| Card payment under review, or a dispute was opened | **On hold** |
| Payment failed, cancelled or expired | **Failed**. The customer can pay again |
| Card payment refunded in Yativo | **Refunded** |

## Keep keys out of the database

Define the credentials in `wp-config.php` instead of the settings page. Constants take priority:

```php wp-config.php
define( 'YATIVO_API_KEY', 'yativo_pk_...' );
define( 'YATIVO_API_SECRET', 'yativo_sk_...' );
define( 'YATIVO_WEBHOOK_SECRET', '...' );
define( 'YATIVO_ENVIRONMENT', 'production' );
```

Saved secrets are never shown again on the settings page, and the debug log never contains them.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Test connection** says the IP isn't allowed | Add your web server's IP to the API key in the Yativo dashboard. |
| **Test connection** says the credentials are invalid | Copy the key and secret again. Sandbox keys only work with **Sandbox** selected. |
| The payment method doesn't show at checkout | Check it's enabled, and that you loaded and ticked methods in your **store currency**. Cards only show in USD stores with a seller customer ID. |
| Orders stay **Pending payment** after paying | Click **Register webhook URL** again, and make sure a security plugin or firewall isn't blocking `/wp-json/yativo/v1/webhook`. |
| "We could not start your payment" | Turn on **Debug log** in **WooCommerce → Settings → Yativo**, try again, and read **WooCommerce → Status → Logs** (source `yativo-payments`). |

Refunds are made in Yativo. Card refunds made there are reflected on the order automatically.
