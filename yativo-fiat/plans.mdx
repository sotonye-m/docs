description: "Choose a plan, pay for it from your USD balance or by card, and pay service fees"

Your account is always on a plan. The plan sets your default [fees](/yativo-fiat/fees), your limits and the features listed on it. These endpoints show the plans you can choose, change or renew your plan, and pay the service fees billed to your account.

Every endpoint acts on your business account, whoever on your team calls it. Team members need the **Plans → view** permission to read and **Plans → upgrade** for everything else.

## How you pay for a plan

Pass `payment_method` on quote, subscribe and renew. It defaults to `wallet`.

| Method | `payment_method` | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| USD balance | `wallet` | The plan price is debited from your **USD** wallet. With `auto_renew` on, each renewal is debited too |
| Card | `card` | You get a `checkout_url` for a secure card payment page. The plan activates once the payment is confirmed. Card billing can be recurring or one-time (see below). **Paying by card doesn't add money to your USD wallet** |

For card, `auto_renew` chooses how the card is billed:

| `auto_renew` | Card billing |
|---|---|
| `true` (default) | **Recurring.** The saved card is charged at the end of every period until you cancel |
| `false` | **One-time.** The card is charged once for this period. The plan ends at `current_period_end` unless you renew it, from your USD balance or by card. `cancel_at_period_end` is `true` once the plan is active |

`GET /business/subscription` returns `payment_options`: each method with a `label`, a `description` to show your users, `available` and `unavailable_reason`. Plans with custom negotiated pricing can only be paid from the USD balance.

When a renewal isn't paid, the plan stays active for a grace period. Pay before `grace_ends_at` (by wallet or card) to keep it. Otherwise the account moves to its fallback plan, usually the free plan.

## Endpoints

| Method | Path | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `GET` | `/business/subscription` | Current plan and billing state |
| `GET` | `/business/subscription/plans` | Plans you can choose |
| `GET` | `/business/subscription/quote` | Cost and effect of choosing a plan |
| `POST` | `/business/subscription/subscribe` | Subscribe, upgrade or downgrade |
| `POST` | `/business/subscription/renew` | Pay for the current plan now |
| `POST` | `/business/subscription/cancel` | Stop renewing at the end of the period |
| `POST` | `/business/subscription/resume` | Undo a cancellation or a scheduled downgrade |
| `PUT` | `/business/subscription/auto-renew` | Turn automatic renewal on or off |
| `GET` | `/business/subscription/payments` | Plan payments |
| `GET` | `/business/subscription/history` | Plan changes and renewals |

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `status` | `active`, `past_due` (a renewal failed; the plan is kept until `grace_ends_at`) or `overdue` (the grace period ended) |

| `scheduled_change` | A downgrade waiting for the end of the period: `plan_id`, `plan_name` and `effective_at` |
| `manage_card_url` | Card billing only. Opens a page to update the card or download card receipts. Valid for 30 minutes; read the subscription again for a fresh link |
| `actions` | Whether you can renew, cancel or resume right now |
| `payment_methods` | The methods you can pass to subscribe and renew |
| `payment_options` | The same methods with display text and availability |

An account with no plan is put on the free plan the first time it calls this endpoint.

## List plans

```
GET /business/subscription/plans
```

```json Response
{
  "status": "success",
  "status_code": 200,
  "message": "Request successful",
  "data": {
    "plans": [
      {
        "id": 2,
        "name": "Pro",
        "description": "For growing businesses",
        "price": 30,
        "currency": "USD",
        "period_days": 30,
        "is_free": false,
        "is_custom": false,
        "contact_sales": false,
        "is_current": true,
        "change_type": "renew",
        "features": [
          { "code": "payouts", "name": "Payouts per month", "description": null, "type": "limit", "limit": 500 }
        ],
        "metadata": null
      }
    ],
    "payment_methods": ["wallet", "card"]
  }
}
```

- `change_type` says what choosing the plan would do: `new`, `renew`, `upgrade` (takes effect now) or `downgrade` (takes effect at the end of the paid period).
- `is_custom: true` marks a plan built for your account with negotiated pricing. Only you see it.
- `contact_sales: true` plans have `price: null` and can't be bought through the API. Contact your account manager.
- A feature `limit` of `-1` means unlimited.

## Get a quote

See what a plan change costs before you make it.

```
GET /business/subscription/quote?plan_id=3&payment_method=wallet
```

```json Response
{
  "status": "success",
  "status_code": 200,
  "message": "Request successful",
  "data": {
    "action": "upgrade",
    "effective": "now",
    "price": 90,
    "credit": 15,
    "amount_due": 75,
    "currency": "USD",
    "period_end": "2026-11-12T10:00:00+00:00",
    "message": "Unused time on your current plan (15.00 USD) is credited against Scale."
  }
}
```

When you upgrade and pay from the USD balance, the unused part of your current paid period is credited (`credit`) and you pay `amount_due`. Card upgrades charge the full price, so `credit` is `0`. A downgrade returns `effective: "period_end"` and `amount_due: 0`.

## Subscribe, upgrade or downgrade

```
POST /business/subscription/subscribe
```

| Field | Required | Description |
|---|---|---|
| `plan_id` | Yes | From `GET /business/subscription/plans` |
| `payment_method` | No | `wallet` (default) or `card`. Ignored for free plans |
| `auto_renew` | No | Defaults to `true`. USD balance: `false` stops automatic renewal from the balance. Card: `false` makes it a one-time payment for this period |

<RequestExample>
```bash Wallet
curl -X POST 'https://api.yativo.com/api/v1/business/subscription/subscribe' \
  -H 'X-Api-Key: YOUR_API_KEY' \
  -H 'X-Api-Secret: YOUR_API_SECRET' \
  -H 'Idempotency-Key: 5d1e8f2a-3b4c-4d6e-9f7a-1b2c3d4e5f60' \
  -H 'Content-Type: application/json' \
  -d '{ "plan_id": 3, "payment_method": "wallet", "auto_renew": true }'
```

```bash Card (recurring)
curl -X POST 'https://api.yativo.com/api/v1/business/subscription/subscribe' \
  -H 'X-Api-Key: YOUR_API_KEY' \
  -H 'X-Api-Secret: YOUR_API_SECRET' \
  -H 'Idempotency-Key: 8a2c4e6f-1b3d-4f5a-8c7e-9d0b1a2c3e4f' \
  -H 'Content-Type: application/json' \
  -d '{ "plan_id": 3, "payment_method": "card", "auto_renew": true }'
```

```bash Card (one-time)

```
</RequestExample>

<ResponseExample>
```json Card
{
  "status": "success",
  "status_code": 200,
  "message": "Complete the payment to activate your plan.",
  "data": {
    "action": "checkout",
    "checkout_url": "https://api.yativo.com/subscriptions/checkout/6f0d3c4e-...",

  }
```

`data.action` says what happened:

| `action` | Meaning | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| `activated` | The new plan is active now and the USD balance was debited | Show the new `subscription` |
| `renewed` | You chose your current plan, so it was renewed for another period | Nothing |
| `scheduled` | A downgrade. You keep the current plan until it ends | Show `subscription.scheduled_change` |
| `checkout` | A card payment is needed. The subscription is unchanged until it's paid | Send the user to `checkout_url` |

### Pay a plan by card

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start the checkout">
    Call `subscribe` (or `renew` for the current plan) with `payment_method: "card"`, and `auto_renew: false` for a one-time payment. Send the user to `data.checkout_url`. The link opens the secure card payment page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The user pays">
    After paying, the user is sent back to **Settings → Billing** in the dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the plan">
    Read `GET /business/subscription` until `billing_method` is `card` and `plan.id` is the plan you chose. This usually takes a few seconds. Plan payments show as `pending` in `GET /business/subscription/payments` until then.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Checkout links expire after 60 minutes. Calling `subscribe` with `card` again for the same plan and the same `auto_renew` returns the open link while it's still valid. To make a one-time card plan recurring, call `resume` before it ends. When a card renewal fails, `status` becomes `past_due`: renew with `card` for a new checkout, or with `wallet`.

## Renew now

Pay for the current plan straight away: renew early (the new period starts when the current one ends) or settle a `past_due` or `overdue` plan.

```bash
curl -X POST 'https://api.yativo.com/api/v1/business/subscription/renew' \
  -H 'X-Api-Key: YOUR_API_KEY' \
  -H 'X-Api-Secret: YOUR_API_SECRET' \
  -H 'Idempotency-Key: 2e4f6a8c-0b1d-4e3f-a5c7-9e1b3d5f7a90' \
  -H 'Content-Type: application/json' \
  -d '{ "payment_method": "wallet" }'
```

Returns `action: "renewed"` for `wallet`, or `action: "checkout"` with a `checkout_url` for `card`. With `card`, send `auto_renew: false` to pay this period once instead of setting up recurring charges. Free plans renew by themselves, so renewing one returns an error.

## Cancel, resume and auto-renew

```bash
curl -X POST 'https://api.yativo.com/api/v1/business/subscription/cancel' \
  -H 'X-Api-Key: YOUR_API_KEY' \
  -H 'X-Api-Secret: YOUR_API_SECRET' \
  -H 'Idempotency-Key: 6c8e0a2b-4d5f-4a7c-9e1b-3d5f7a9c1e20' \
  -H 'Content-Type: application/json' \
  -d '{ "reason": "Switching provider" }'
```

- **Cancel:** `reason` is optional. The plan stays active until `current_period_end`, then the account moves to the free plan. Nothing more is charged, and `cancel_at_period_end` becomes `true`.
- **Resume:** `POST /business/subscription/resume` turns renewal back on and drops a scheduled downgrade. For a one-time card plan it makes the card billing recurring. It only works before the period ends.
- **Auto-renew:** `PUT /business/subscription/auto-renew` with `{ "auto_renew": false }`. For USD balance billing, the plan then isn't renewed automatically; renew it yourself before it ends. For card billing it's the same as cancel and resume.

## Payment and plan history

```
GET /business/subscription/payments?per_page=20
GET /business/subscription/history?per_page=20
```

Both are paginated, newest first: the rows are in `data` and the page is in `pagination` (`current_page`, `last_page`, `per_page`, `total`). Each payment has `id`, `status` (`succeeded`, `failed`, `pending` or `refunded`), `method`, `amount`, `currency`, `plan`, `invoice_number`, `period_start`, `period_end`, `failure_reason`, `paid_at` and `created_at`.

Each history entry has a `type`, `from_plan`, `to_plan`, `by` (`You`, `Yativo` or `Automatic`) and `created_at`. `type` is one of `started`, `subscribed`, `upgraded`, `renewed`, `assigned`, `change_scheduled`, `cancel_scheduled`, `resumed`, `past_due`, `recovered`, `overdue`, `downgraded`, `ended`, `fallback`, `invoice_paid`, `extended`, `auto_renew_on` or `auto_renew_off`.

The account owner is emailed when a plan activates or renews, a few days before a renewal, when a renewal fails, when a plan becomes overdue or is downgraded, and when a cancellation or downgrade is scheduled.

## Service fees

Some services are billed separately from your plan: account maintenance and the annual verification (KYC/KYB) review. Each charge is a **bill**. Bills aren't transactions and never appear in your transaction history. Yativo collects outstanding bills from your USD balance when it covers them. You can also pay them by card.

| Method | Path | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `GET` | `/business/billing/pending` | Everything still owed, oldest due first |
| `GET` | `/business/billing/partially-paid` | Bills that are partly paid |
| `GET` | `/business/billing/history` | Every bill, newest first |
| `GET` | `/business/billing/{id}` | One bill with its line items and payments |
| `GET` | `/business/billing/{id}/line-items` | What the bill is for, per account or service |
| `GET` | `/business/billing/{id}/collections` | Every payment made against the bill |
| `POST` | `/business/billing/card-payments` | Pay outstanding bills by card |
| `GET` | `/business/billing/card-payments/{reference}` | Status of a card payment |

### Pay bills by card

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a card payment">
    Send the IDs of the bills to pay (1 to 50). The payment covers their full outstanding balance.

    ```bash
    curl -X POST 'https://api.yativo.com/api/v1/business/billing/card-payments' \
      -H 'X-Api-Key: YOUR_API_KEY' \
      -H 'X-Api-Secret: YOUR_API_SECRET' \
      -H 'Idempotency-Key: 4b6d8f0a-2c3e-4a5b-8d7f-1a3c5e7b9d20' \
      -H 'Content-Type: application/json' \
      -d '{ "bill_ids": ["9a4f2c1e-6b3d-4e8a-b7c5-1d2e3f4a5b6c"] }'
    ```

    ```json Response
    {
      "status": "success",
      "status_code": 201,
      "message": "Request successful",
      "data": {
        "reference": "bcp_01jbx7q2k9x0m3c8v1b5n6z4l2",
        "status": "initiated",
        "amount": 25,
        "currency": "USD",
        "checkout_url": "https://...",
        "bills": [{ "billing_record_id": "9a4f2c1e-6b3d-4e8a-b7c5-1d2e3f4a5b6c", "amount": 25 }],
        "failure_reason": null,
        "paid_at": null,
        "expires_at": "2026-11-05T15:00:00+00:00",
        "created_at": "2026-11-05T14:00:00+00:00"
      }
    }
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the user to checkout_url">
    The user pays on the secure card payment page and is sent back to **Settings → Billing** in the dashboard. Keep the `reference`: the return doesn't include it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the payment">
    Read `GET /business/billing/card-payments/{reference}` until `status` is `succeeded`. A bill only counts as paid once its card payment has succeeded.
  </Step>
</Steps>

| Card payment `status` | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `initiated` | Waiting for the user to pay. `checkout_url` is set |
| `processing` | Payment submitted and waiting for confirmation. `checkout_url` is set |
| `succeeded` | Paid. The bills are settled |
| `failed` | The payment didn't go through. `failure_reason` says why |
| `expired` | The link expired before payment. Start a new card payment |

The link expires after `expires_at`, 60 minutes by default. Starting a card payment again for the same bills while one is open returns the open one.

## Errors

Plan and billing errors return `422` with a message you can show the user. See [Error response format](/fiat-api-reference/introduction#error-response-format) for the full response.

| Message | Cause |
|---|---|
| `Insufficient USD wallet balance: ...` | Add USD to your wallet, or pay with `payment_method: "card"` |
| `Please contact sales to move to the Enterprise plan.` | The plan has `contact_sales: true` |
| `This plan is not available.` | The plan is inactive or belongs to another account |
| `Card payments are not available right now. Pay from your wallet instead.` | Card payment is temporarily unavailable |
| `Free plans renew automatically.` | You called `renew` on a free plan |
| `Select at least one bill to pay.` | `bill_ids` is empty |
| `One or more selected bills are not outstanding.` | A bill in `bill_ids` is already paid, cancelled or not yours |
| `Card checkout could not be started. Please try again.` | The card payment page couldn't be opened. Retry |

## Older plan endpoints

`GET /business/plans/current`, `GET /business/plans/all`, `POST /business/plans/subscribe/{plan_id}` and `PUT /business/plans/upgrade/{plan_id}` still work and follow the same rules: USD balance by default, or pass `payment_method: "card"` to get a `checkout_url`. Use the `/business/subscription` endpoints for new work.
