---
title: "Get verification form"
description: "Prefill, missing fields and allowed business structures"
openapi: "openapi-fiat.yaml GET /card-checkout/customers/{customer_id}/verification"
---

Use this when you collect business details in your own app. Individual customers add their ID and selfie on the onboarding page. The tax identification number is never prefilled.

See [Verify a business customer with your own form](/yativo-fiat/card-checkout#or-verify-a-business-customer-with-your-own-form).

<RequestExample>
```bash cURL
curl 'https://api.yativo.com/api/v1/card-checkout/customers/4f0c1e1a-8d2b-4c7e-9b1a-3e5f6a7b8c9d/verification' \
  -H 'X-Api-Key: YOUR_API_KEY' \
  -H 'X-Api-Secret: YOUR_API_SECRET'
```
</RequestExample>
