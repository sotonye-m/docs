---
title: "List Swap Pairs"
description: "The currency pairs you can swap between your wallets, with rates, fees and limits"
openapi: "openapi-fiat.yaml GET /wallet/swap/pairs"
---

Returns every pair you can swap. Each pair has its own rate, fees and amount limits. See [Currency Swap](/yativo-fiat/swap) for how the fee and `to_amount` are calculated.
