---
title: "Submit Business KYB"
description: "Submit identity and compliance verification data for a business customer"
openapi: "POST https://kyc.yativo.com/api/business-kyc/submit"
---

Submit KYB data for a business customer. The customer must already exist with `customer_type: "business"` (created with `POST /customer`), or the request returns `404 Business with the provided Customer_ID not found.`

A successful response means the data passed validation and was accepted. Verification runs afterwards, so the customer stays pending until [their status](/yativo-fiat/kyc#check-kyc--kyb-status) changes. Every problem is reported in one `422` response. See [KYC / KYB Verification](/yativo-fiat/kyc#error-handling) for the error shape.

<Note>
  This endpoint is on the KYC platform at `https://kyc.yativo.com`, not the main API base URL. Every file field accepts a hosted URL, a base64 data URI or a `multipart/form-data` upload. Files must be PDF, JPG, JPEG, PNG, HEIC or TIF, between 5 KB and 30 MB.
</Note>

```
POST https://kyc.yativo.com/api/business-kyc/submit
```

## Business identity

<ParamField body="customer_id" type="string" required>
  The customer ID returned by `POST /customer`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="business_legal_name" type="string" required>
  Registered legal name. Max 255 characters.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="business_trade_name" type="string" required>
  Trade or DBA name. Max 255 characters.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="business_description" type="string" required>
  What the business does. 140–5000 characters.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="email" type="string" required>
  Business contact email.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="business_type" type="string" required>
  `cooperative`, `corporation`, `llc`, `partnership`, `sole_prop`, `trust` or `other`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="registration_number" type="string" required>
  Government registration or incorporation number. Max 100 characters.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="incorporation_date" type="string" required>
  `YYYY-MM-DD`, before today.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="primary_website" type="string" required>
  Website URL including the scheme, for example `https://acme.com`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="tax_id" type="string">
  Business tax ID. Required unless you send `identifying_information`. Letters and digits only, up to 255 characters.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="incorporation_country" type="string">
  ISO 3166-1 alpha-2 country code.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="phone_calling_code" type="string">
  Must match `^\+[1-9]\d{0,3}$`, for example `"+1"`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="phone_number" type="string">
  Digits only, 7–15 characters.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="business_industry" type="string">
  Industry, from `GET /business-industries`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="is_dao" type="boolean">
  Whether the business is a decentralized autonomous organization.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="statement_descriptor" type="string">
  How the name appears on statements. Max 22 characters.
</ParamField>

## Addresses

<ParamField body="registered_address" type="object" required>
  The registered address. `physical_address` has the same fields and is also required.

  <Expandable title="address fields">
    <ParamField body="street_line_1" type="string" required>Max 255 characters.</ParamField>
    <ParamField body="street_line_2" type="string">Max 255 characters.</ParamField>
    <ParamField body="city" type="string" required>Max 100 characters.</ParamField>
    <ParamField body="state" type="string" required>Subdivision code from `GET /business-kyc/subdivisions/{countryCode}`, for example `"FL"`.</ParamField>
    <ParamField body="postal_code" type="string" required>Must match the country's format.</ParamField>
    <ParamField body="country" type="string" required>ISO 3166-1 alpha-2.</ParamField>
    <ParamField body="proof_of_address_file" type="file" required>Proof of address for this location.</ParamField>
  </Expandable>
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="physical_address" type="object" required>
  The operating address. Same fields as `registered_address`.
</ParamField>

## Associated persons

<ParamField body="associated_persons" type="array" required>
  Owners, directors and authorized signers. At least one person must own at least `ownership_threshold` percent (default 25). People below the threshold are dropped from the submission. The combined `ownership_percentage` of people with `has_ownership: true` can't exceed 100.

  <Expandable title="associated_persons item fields">
    <ParamField body="first_name" type="string" required>Max 100 characters.</ParamField>
    <ParamField body="last_name" type="string" required>Max 100 characters.</ParamField>
    <ParamField body="birth_date" type="string" required>`YYYY-MM-DD`. The person must be at least 18.</ParamField>
    <ParamField body="nationality" type="string" required>ISO 3166-1 alpha-2.</ParamField>
    <ParamField body="email" type="string" required>Personal email.</ParamField>
    <ParamField body="ownership_percentage" type="number" required>0–100.</ParamField>
    <ParamField body="residential_address" type="object" required>Same fields as `registered_address`, including `proof_of_address_file`.</ParamField>
    <ParamField body="identifying_information" type="array" required>
      At least one entry that isn't a tax ID.

      <Expandable title="identifying_information item fields">
        <ParamField body="type" type="string" required>For example `passport`, `national_id`, `drivers_license` or `tax_id`.</ParamField>
        <ParamField body="number" type="string" required>Document number. For `tax_id`, letters and digits only.</ParamField>
        <ParamField body="issuing_country" type="string">ISO 3166-1 alpha-2.</ParamField>
        <ParamField body="expiration" type="string">`YYYY-MM-DD`, after today.</ParamField>
        <ParamField body="image_front" type="file" required>Front of the document.</ParamField>
        <ParamField body="image_back" type="file">Back of the document. Required except for `passport` and `tax_id`.</ParamField>
      </Expandable>
    </ParamField>
    <ParamField body="phone" type="string">Digits only, 7–15 characters.</ParamField>
    <ParamField body="title" type="string">For example `"CEO"`.</ParamField>
    <ParamField body="tax_id" type="string">Personal tax ID. Letters and digits only.</ParamField>
    <ParamField body="annual_salary" type="string">Up to 20 characters.</ParamField>
    <ParamField body="relationship_established_at" type="string">`YYYY-MM-DD`, not in the future.</ParamField>
    <ParamField body="has_ownership" type="boolean">Has an ownership stake.</ParamField>
    <ParamField body="has_control" type="boolean">Has operational control.</ParamField>
    <ParamField body="is_signer" type="boolean">Authorized signer. A signer who isn't a director requires a `board_resolution` in `extra_documents`.</ParamField>
    <ParamField body="is_director" type="boolean">Director.</ParamField>
  </Expandable>
</ParamField>

## Risk and purpose

<ParamField body="account_purpose" type="string" required>
  One of `charitable_donations`, `ecommerce_retail_payments`, `investment_purposes`, `other`, `payments_to_friends_or_family_abroad`, `payroll`, `personal_or_living_expenses`, `protect_wealth`, `purchase_goods_and_services`, `receive_payments_for_goods_and_services`, `tax_optimization`, `third_party_money_transmission` or `treasury_management`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="account_purpose_other" type="string">
  Required when `account_purpose` is `other`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="source_of_funds" type="string" required>
  PascalCase. One of `BusinessLoans`, `Grants`, `InterCompanyFunds`, `InvestmentProceeds`, `LegalSettlement`, `OwnersCapital`, `PensionRetirement`, `SaleOfAssets`, `SalesOfGoodsAndServices`, `ThirdPartyFunds` or `TreasuryReserves`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="expected_monthly_payments_usd" type="integer" required>
  Expected monthly volume in USD. At least 1.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="high_risk_activities" type="array" required>
  Any of `adult_entertainment`, `gambling`, `hold_client_funds`, `investment_services`, `lending_banking`, `marijuana_or_related_services`, `money_services`, `nicotine_tobacco_or_related_services`, `operate_foreign_exchange_virtual_currencies_brokerage_otc`, `pharmaceuticals`, `precious_metals_precious_stones_jewelry`, `safe_deposit_box_rentals`, `third_party_payment_processing`, `weapons_firearms_and_explosives`. Send `["none_of_the_above"]` if none apply.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="high_risk_activities_explanation" type="string">
  Required if any high-risk activity is listed. Max 5000 characters.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="pep_status" type="boolean" required>
  Whether any associated person is a politically exposed person.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="third_party_msb_payments" type="boolean" required>
  Whether the business makes payments for third-party money services businesses.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="conducts_money_services" type="boolean">
  When `true`, `conducts_money_services_description` and `compliance_screening_explanation` are required.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="ownership_threshold" type="integer">
  5–100. Default 25.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="estimated_annual_revenue_usd" type="string">
  Estimated annual revenue in USD.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="operates_in_prohibited_countries" type="string">
  `yes` or `no`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="has_material_intermediary_ownership" type="boolean">
  Whether the ownership includes material intermediary entities.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="regulated_activity" type="object">
  `regulated_activities_description`, `primary_regulatory_authority_country`, `primary_regulatory_authority_name` and `license_number`, if the business is regulated.
</ParamField>

## Documents

<ParamField body="documents" type="array" required>
  At least one document.

  <Expandable title="documents item fields">
    <ParamField body="purpose" type="string" required>`business_registration`, `proof_of_address`, `tax_documents`, `compliance_documents`, `financial_statements`, `certificate_of_good_standing`, `portfolio_statement` or `board_minutes`.</ParamField>
    <ParamField body="description" type="string" required>For example `"Certificate of Incorporation"`.</ParamField>
    <ParamField body="file" type="file" required>The document.</ParamField>
  </Expandable>
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="extra_documents" type="array" required>
  Items of `{ "type": "...", "file": ... }`. Must include `shareholding_structure`, and `board_resolution` when an authorized signer isn't a director.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="identifying_information" type="array">
  Business-level documents with `type`, `issuing_country`, `number`, `description`, `expiration`, `image_front` and `image_back`. `image_front` is required when `type` is `tax_id`.
</ParamField>
