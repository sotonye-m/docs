---
title: "Cancel Employee Email Change"
description: "Drop an email change that's waiting for the employee"
openapi: "openapi-fiat.yaml DELETE /corporate-card/employees/{employee_id}/email/pending"
---

When an employee has confirmed their email on a card link, an email change only applies once the employee confirms it on their card link. See [Change an employee's email](/yativo-fiat/corporate-card-links#change-an-employees-email).
