---
title: "Get Card Link"
description: "The card's reusable, employee-only page for card details and PIN"
openapi: "openapi-fiat.yaml GET /corporate-card/employees/cards/{card_id}/link"
---

Returns the card's link. Only the card's employee can open it, and it's the only place the card number, CVV and PIN are available. Share it or embed it in an iframe. See [Corporate Card Links](/yativo-fiat/corporate-card-links) for the employee flow, embedding and branding.
