---
title: "Get Employee Card Sign-in"
description: "An employee's card link sign-in status"
openapi: "openapi-fiat.yaml GET /corporate-card/employees/{employee_id}/card-access"
---

Shows whether the employee has confirmed their email, which sign-in methods they use, whether they're locked, and any email change waiting for them. See [Employee sign-in](/yativo-fiat/corporate-card-links#employee-sign-in).
