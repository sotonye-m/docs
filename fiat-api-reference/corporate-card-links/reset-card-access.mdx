---
title: "Reset Employee Card Sign-in"
description: "Let an employee set up their card sign-in again after losing their phone or passkey"
openapi: "openapi-fiat.yaml POST /corporate-card/employees/{employee_id}/card-access/reset"
---

Removes the employee's passkeys and authenticator app and unlocks their card links. They confirm their email and set up sign-in again the next time they open a card link. The employee gets an email telling them their sign-in was reset.
