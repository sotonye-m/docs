## Payments

- **Payouts:** `POST /sendmoney`, `POST /sendmoney/quote` and `POST /sendmoney/purpose` are no longer in the docs. Quote with `POST /exchange-rate` and pay out with `POST /wallet/payout` or `POST /payout/simple`. See [Payouts](/yativo-fiat/payouts)
- **Get a deposit:** new `GET /wallet/deposits/{depositId}` returns one deposit by its `id` or `deposit_id`, so you can confirm its `status` before you fulfil an order. See [Get Deposit](/fiat-api-reference/deposits/get)
- **Card payments:** customers can pay deposits by card on a secure card page, in Bolivia (BOB or USD) and internationally (USD). Card payments are credited in USD and held for 3 US business days before they reach your card balance. See [Card payments](/yativo-fiat/deposits#card-payments)
- **Ecuador payouts:** send USD bank transfers to Ecuador, from 1.00 to 10,000.00 USD with cents. See [Ecuador (USD)](/yativo-fiat/beneficiaries#ecuador-usd)
- **EUR deposits via SEPA:** customers can fund deposits by bank transfer to a Yativo IBAN. See [Deposits](/yativo-fiat/deposits#eur-bank-transfer-sepa)
- **Pay out by receive amount:** set `amount_type: "receive"` on `POST /exchange-rate` (payout quotes) or `POST /wallet/payout` to fix exactly what the customer receives. Yativo works out the send amount and fees. `POST /wallet/payout` responses now include `amount_type`, `send_amount`, `customer_receive_amount` and `debit_amount`. See [Pay out by receive amount](/yativo-fiat/exchange-rates#pay-out-by-receive-amount)
- **Market rates:** new `GET /exchange-rates` returns live indicative rates for every wallet currency, for display to your customers. See [Live market rates](/yativo-fiat/exchange-rates#live-market-rates)
- **Fee schedule:** new `GET /fees` returns your account's fees for every payin and payout method, virtual account currency and card action, including custom pricing. See [Fees](/yativo-fiat/fees)
- **Currency swap:** swap balance between your own wallets with `GET /wallet/swap/pairs`, `POST /wallet/swap/quote`, `POST /wallet/swap`, `GET /wallet/swap` and `GET /wallet/swap/{reference}`. Each pair has its own rate, fees and limits. `POST /swap/init` and `POST /swap/process` are no longer available. See [Currency Swap](/yativo-fiat/swap)
- **Beneficiaries are saved payment methods:** add a beneficiary with `POST /beneficiaries/payment-methods` using `gateway_id`, `payment_data`, `currency` and `nickname`, and use its `id` in payouts and quotes. You no longer need to create a separate beneficiary record first. See [Beneficiaries](/yativo-fiat/beneficiaries)
- **Payout references and invoices:** `POST /wallet/payout` and `POST /payout/simple` accept `reference` (shown to the recipient's bank), plus `invoice` or `invoice_url`. `payout.updated` webhooks include `reference`. See [Initiate Payout](/fiat-api-reference/payments/payout)
- **NGN rates:** NGN is priced at the average market rate, or the buy rate when no average is available, across quotes, deposits, payouts and `GET /exchange-rates`
- **Gift cards discontinued:** `GET /giftcards`, `GET /giftcards/categories`, `POST /giftcards`, `GET /giftcards/redeem/{transactionId}` and the `giftcard.status_changed` webhook are no longer available
- **`virtual_account.deleted` webhook:** sent when you delete a virtual account. `DELETE /business/virtual-account/delete-virtual-account/{account_id}` now returns the deleted `account_id`, and `404` for an ID that isn't yours. See [Delete Virtual Account](/fiat-api-reference/virtual-accounts/delete)
- **Virtual account currencies:** `USD_KIRA` and `USDKIRA` are no longer available. Get the codes your account can use from `GET /business/virtual-account/currencies-and-endorsements`
- **Wallet address limits:** `POST /crypto/create-wallet` and corporate card funding addresses allow 3 address requests a minute per customer. Going over returns `403` and blocks both for that customer for 24 hours. See [Wallet address limits](/yativo-fiat/deposits#wallet-address-limits)

## Cards

- **Virtual cards:** `POST /customer/virtual/cards/activate` is no longer in the docs. Issue a card with `POST /customer/virtual/cards/create`. See [Virtual Cards](/yativo-fiat/virtual-cards)
- **Virtual cards:** every card request works only on your own cards. Any other card ID returns `404` with `Card not found`. See [Virtual Cards](/yativo-fiat/virtual-cards)
- **Corporate cards in sandbox (invitation only):** issue cards to your business customers' employees, fund each employee's balance from your USDC balance, and review memos, in the sandbox API and dashboard ahead of production. See [Corporate Cards in Sandbox](/yativo-fiat/sandbox-corporate-cards)
- **Corporate card links:** card number, CVV and PIN are now only on each card's reusable, embeddable link, which only the card's employee can open (email code the first time, then a passkey or authenticator app). Brand it with your logo, font and colours, reset an employee's sign-in, and change an employee's email with their confirmation on the card link. Removed: `GET /spend/cards/{card_id}/widget-token`, `POST /spend/cards/pin` and `POST /corporate-card/employees/cards/pin`. See [Corporate Card Links](/yativo-fiat/corporate-card-links)
- **Corporate card errors:** a failed card request returns only `data.error` with a readable reason. When the card service can't complete the request, you get `502`. See [Errors](/yativo-fiat/sandbox-corporate-cards#errors)
- **USDC balance:** your USDC balance is only for corporate cards. Payouts, transfers and swaps out of it fail with `Invalid wallet selected.` Fund it with a USDC deposit or a swap from USD. See [USDC balance](/yativo-fiat/sandbox-corporate-cards#usdc-balance)
- **Corporate cards:** customers now need an approved `cobo_pobo` endorsement, not `faster_payments`

## KYC

- **KYB tax ID documents:** a `tax_id` entry in an associated person's or the business's `identifying_information` now needs `image_front`: a photo of the tax ID or a tax return filing. Tax ID numbers take letters and digits only. See [Business KYB](/yativo-fiat/kyc#business-kyb)
- **KYB associated persons:** directors, signers and controllers (`is_director`, `is_signer` or `has_control` true) are now kept even when they own less than `ownership_threshold`. At least one person must still meet the threshold. The role flags accept `true`/`false`, `1`/`0` and `"1"`/`"0"`. See [Business KYB](/yativo-fiat/kyc#business-kyb)
- **Hosted KYB drafts:** customers can save any section of the hosted business form as a draft and finish it later. They can't submit until every drafted section is saved. See [Integration approaches](/yativo-fiat/kyc#integration-approaches)
- **Verification results come from endorsements:** a customer's KYC or KYB result is only available through their endorsements, via the `endorsement.updated` webhook or the `endorsement` list on `GET /customer/{customer_id}`. Each service is available when its endorsement is `approved`. `GET /customer/kyc/{customer_id}` and `is_va_approved` are no longer documented as a status check. See [Track verification with endorsements](/yativo-fiat/kyc#track-verification-with-endorsements)
- **Endorsement resubmission:** `POST /customer/kyc/resubmit/{customer_id}/{endorsement}` re-runs verification for one endorsement. The `{endorsement}` path value is no longer case-sensitive. See [KYC / KYB](/yativo-fiat/kyc#resubmit-one-endorsement)
- **KYC sandbox host:** sandbox customers use `https://kyc-sandbox.yativo.com`; production uses `https://kyc.yativo.com`. KYC platform endpoints don't need an API key. KYB business profile fields go in the `extra_business_info`, `collections_data` and `payouts_data` objects. See [KYC / KYB](/yativo-fiat/kyc)
- **KYC lookups in the API reference:** countries, subdivisions, postal code formats, ID types, occupations, industries, account purposes, sources of funds and transaction currencies and countries, each with a "Try it" playground. See [KYC Lookups](/fiat-api-reference/kyc-lookups/countries)
- **KYC rules documented:** duplicate-person `409` with `duplicate_of`, the permanent `403` for placeholder names, what each endorsement needs, sandbox test values, and a go-live checklist. Files can be 5 KB–30 MB; tax IDs take letters and digits only; KYB requires `primary_website`, proof of address for every address, `pep_status`, `third_party_msb_payments`, `expected_monthly_payments_usd` and a `shareholding_structure` extra document; associated persons below the ownership threshold (default 25%) are dropped; individual submissions return `warnings` for values verification is likely to reject. See [KYC / KYB](/yativo-fiat/kyc)

## Sandbox

- **Sandbox simulation endpoints:** credit a wallet, settle a payin or payout, simulate a virtual-account deposit, fire a virtual-card or spend-card event, or send a sample webhook. All of them live in the [Sandbox](/fiat-api-reference/sandbox/wallet-topup) API reference group. Production returns `404 Not found`. See [Sandbox](/yativo-fiat/sandbox#sandbox-simulation-endpoints)
- **IP whitelist optional on sandbox:** sandbox API keys may omit allowed IPs. Production (`api.yativo.com` and every other host on that server) still requires a whitelist — a request with none is `401`. See [IP whitelisting](/yativo-fiat/ip-allowlist)
- **Sandbox KYC:** sandbox does not approve KYC when you create a customer. Your account stays limited until `kyc_status` is `approved` and `is_kyc_submitted` is true. The sandbox dashboard collects business verification in a form. See [Sandbox KYC](/yativo-fiat/sandbox-kyc)

## API

- **Official SDKs:** libraries for Node.js, Python, PHP, Laravel and Go, and a WooCommerce plugin. They handle API key authentication, idempotency keys, safe retries, typed errors and webhook verification. See [SDKs](/yativo-fiat/sdks/overview)
- **Login tokens discontinued:** `POST /auth/login`, `GET /auth/refresh-token` and `Authorization: Bearer` tokens (legacy Account ID and App Secret) no longer work for API requests. Send `X-Api-Key` and `X-Api-Secret` on every request. See [Authentication](/yativo-fiat/authentication)
- **Webhook payloads:** `user.updated`, `customer.created`, `customer.updated` and `deposit.*` now carry only their documented fields. `user.updated` is sent when your name, email, membership ID or KYC status changes, not when you sign in. `customer.updated` is sent when a profile field or the customer's KYC status changes. `endorsement.updated` no longer includes `_webhook_id`. The delivery log and resends show each payload with today's fields. See [Webhooks](/yativo-fiat/webhooks)
- **Unified errors:** every failed response now includes `error_details`, with a stable `code`, `message`, `fields`, `retryable` and `retry_after`. See [Error response format](/fiat-api-reference/introduction#error-response-format)
- **MCP server:** connect Claude, Cursor or any MCP client to your fiat account at `https://api.yativo.com/mcp` to check balances and deposits, and quote and send payouts with your approval. See [Fiat MCP server](/yativo-fiat/mcp-server)
- Endpoints that change sign-in settings (passkeys, two-factor authentication, PIN, app secret) or delete the account now return `403` when called with an API key
- `GET /ip-whitelist/check` now returns `api_key.label` (was `name`) and a `network.warning` when your request came through a proxy
- **Try it on every endpoint:** every fiat API reference page now has an interactive playground
- **More endpoints in the API reference:** corporate card employees, employee KYC submission, employee balances, cards, memos, the USDC funding address and card transactions, plus stablecoin deposit wallets, pay-in countries and currencies, `GET /currencies/all`, `GET /business/transaction/by-currency`, `POST /storage/upload` and `DELETE /customer/{customer_id}`. Each has a "Try it" playground. See [Corporate Cards](/fiat-api-reference/corporate-cards/employees-create)

## Docs

- **Corrected:** the fiat webhook signature header is `Signature`, not `X-Yativo-Signature`. Webhooks time out after 3 seconds and are attempted up to 3 times. The [Webhooks](/yativo-fiat/webhooks) page now lists every event type, including virtual card and corporate card events
- **Corrected:** `POST /payout/simple` requires `debit_wallet` and `customer_id`. Insufficient balance returns `400`, not `402`. Idempotency keys are kept for 60 minutes, not 24 hours
- **Corrected:** KYB `account_purpose` and `source_of_funds` use their own lists, separate from individual KYC. KYB associated persons send ID images as `image_front` and `image_back`
- **Corrected:** `GET /payment-methods/payin/currency` takes a three-letter `country` (`BRA`, not `BR`), and `GET /payment-methods/payin/countries` returns `country`, `name`, `flag` and `currencies`. The `GET /corporate-card/wallet` balances are in USD, not cents
- [AI and MCP servers](/yativo/mcp) compares the Docs, Fiat and Agentic Wallet MCP servers, and documents the Docs MCP server at `https://docs.yativo.com/mcp`

## Yativo Crypto

- Card IBAN removed from the crypto docs. To receive money by bank transfer, use [Yativo Fiat virtual accounts](/yativo-fiat/virtual-accounts)

<Update label="September 2026" description="API keys, IP whitelisting, and passkeys" tags={["Fiat", "Crypto"]}>

## Yativo Fiat

- **API Keys:** servers now authenticate with `X-Api-Key` and `X-Api-Secret`. See [Authentication](/yativo-fiat/authentication)
- **Deprecated:** legacy API keys (Account ID + App Secret, `POST /auth/login`). From 15 October 2026, its tokens only work inside the dashboard
- **IP whitelisting:** every API key lists the server IPs allowed to use it, enforced from midnight on 14 October 2026. See [IP whitelisting](/yativo-fiat/ip-allowlist)
- New `GET /ip-whitelist/check` to confirm each server is allowed
- **Passkeys:** everyone on a team can sign in to the dashboard with a passkey. See [Passkeys](/yativo-fiat/passkeys)

## Yativo Crypto

- Customers: get, update and delete a customer, plus per-customer auto-forwarding. See [Customers](/yativo-crypto/customers)
- Agentic wallets: per-connector capabilities, `GET /agent/capabilities`, `GET /agent/openapi.json`, and prediction market trading. See [Agentic Wallets](/yativo-crypto/agentic-wallets)
- Transactions: `POST /transactions/preview-fee`, daily and monthly withdrawal limits, and review holds for large sends
- Gas station low-balance email alerts with custom thresholds and recipients
- Swap: `GET /swap/destinations` and `GET /swap/available-chains`
- Deposit webhooks use the on-chain transaction hash as `transaction_id`; deposits below the minimum aren't credited
- Secure card view: card data always renders light, and the view can be embedded on any website
- Crypto docs now cover the crypto API only. Fiat features are documented under Yativo Fiat

</Update>
