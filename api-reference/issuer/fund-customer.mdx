---
title: "Fund Customer"
api: "POST /v1/card-issuer/fund-customer"
description: "Fund a customer's card wallet on their behalf as a card issuer"
authMethod: "bearer"
---

<ParamField header="Authorization" type="string" required>
  Bearer token: `Bearer YOUR_ACCESS_TOKEN`
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="customer_id" type="string">
  The customer's Yativo customer ID. One of `customer_id`, `card_id`, `external_id`, or `email` is required.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="card_id" type="string">
  The card's Gnosis card ID. Alternative identifier.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="external_id" type="string">
  Your own external reference set at customer onboarding. Alternative identifier.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="email" type="string">
  The customer's email address. Alternative identifier.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="amount" type="number" required>
  Amount to fund. Interpretation depends on `pricing_mode`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="source_chain" type="string" required>
  Which master wallet to send from. One of: `SOL` or `XDC`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="pricing_mode" type="string">
  How the `amount` is interpreted. `send_x` (default) — you specify what you send, customer receives less after fees. `receive_x` — you specify what the customer receives, more is taken from your master wallet.
</ParamField>

<RequestExample>
```bash
curl -X POST 'https://crypto-api.yativo.com/api/v1/card-issuer/fund-customer' \
  -H 'Authorization: Bearer YOUR_ACCESS_TOKEN' \
  -H 'Content-Type: application/json' \
  -d '{
    "customer_id": "cust_abc123",
    "amount": 500,
    "source_chain": "SOL",
    "pricing_mode": "receive_x"
  }'
```
</RequestExample>
