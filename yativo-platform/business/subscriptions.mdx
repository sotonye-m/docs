---
title: "Plans & Subscriptions"
description: "Manage your plan, subscriptions, and billing in the Yativo dashboard."
---

Plans, subscriptions, and billing are managed in the [Yativo dashboard](https://app.yativo.com) under **Settings → Billing** and are not available via the API.

To read the transaction fees that apply to your current plan via the API, see [Fee Schedule](/yativo-platform/money-movement/fees).
