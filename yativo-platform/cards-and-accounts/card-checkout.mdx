Card Checkout lets your customers accept debit and credit card payments on a
secure, Yativo-hosted checkout page. Each of your customers that sells is
verified once through its `card_checkout` endorsement. You then create orders
for it through the API, send the buyer to the returned `checkout_url`, and
receive a webhook for every change in the order.

Each card payment lands on the **selling customer's own** connected business account (`biz_…` from card-checkout onboarding).

Yativo collects its fee from the seller's account (processor fee, plus 1%, plus a
fixed fee). The rest stays on the customer's account. They can withdraw **only
the amount that has cleared on Yativo** (`available`). Save a payout destination
with `POST /v1/card-checkout/customers/{customer_id}/payout-methods`, then
withdraw with `POST /v1/card-checkout/customers/{customer_id}/payouts`.

## Before you start

**Card Checkout must be enabled for your account.** Request access with
[`POST /v1/card-checkout/account/request-access`](#request-access); our team
reviews the request. Submitting a customer's endorsement before then also files
the request for you (see [Before your account is enabled](#before-your-account-is-enabled)).
Until access is approved, the other endpoints below return `403` with
`code: card_checkout_not_enabled`. Your pricing (fee, settlement delay, reserve
and currencies) is set when access is granted and is shown by
[`GET /v1/card-checkout/account`](#check-your-account).

### Request access

```http
POST /v1/card-checkout/account/request-access
Content-Type: application/json

{ "note": "We sell online courses to customers in the US and EU." }
```

`note` is optional (max 1000 characters): tell us what you sell and to whom.
The response is your account object with `access_status: "requested"` (HTTP
`202`). Sending the request again while it is open changes nothing. If access is
already enabled you get `200` with `access_status: "enabled"`.

The status codes in this guide are the `status_code` in the response envelope.

| `access_status` | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `requested` | Waiting for review. |
| `enabled` | Approved. Endorse the customers who will sell next. |
| `declined` | Not approved. You can send a new request with more detail. |
| `disabled` | Access was turned off. Contact support, or send a new request. |

Every request needs your API credentials, and your Yativo account must have
passed KYC:

```http
X-Api-Key: <YOUR_API_KEY>
X-Api-Secret: <YOUR_API_SECRET>
```

Send an `Idempotency-Key` header on every `POST` so a retried request is never
executed twice. See [Payin & Payout Integration → Idempotency](/yativo-platform/money-movement/payins#idempotency).

All responses use the standard envelope:
`{ "status": "success" | "failed", "status_code": ..., "message": "...", "data": { ... } }`.

Amounts in requests and responses are **decimal strings in major units**
(`"49.99"`). Currency codes are ISO 4217; only **USD** is enabled today.

## How it works

```
Your server ──POST /v1/card-checkout/customers/{id}/endorsement──▶ Yativo  (once per selling customer)
Customer    ──▶ onboarding_url ──▶ confirms details, adds ID / company documents
Your server ◀── webhook endorsement.updated (card_checkout: approved) ── Yativo

Your server ──POST /v1/card-checkout/orders { customer_id, amount }──▶ Yativo  (amount fixed here)
Your server ◀──────── checkout_url ──────────── Yativo
Buyer       ──▶ checkout_url ──▶ pays by card (3-D Secure if the bank asks)
Your server ◀── webhook card_checkout.order.paid ── Yativo   (fulfil here)
Yativo      ── the order's net amount ──▶ your balance, recorded against the customer
```

- The amount is set when **you** create the order. The buyer cannot change it.
- Card details are entered in secure, isolated fields; they never reach your
  servers or ours.
- **Only the `card_checkout.order.paid` webhook means the payment was made.**
  The buyer's browser being redirected to your `success_url` is not proof of
  payment: browsers close, redirects fail, and URLs can be typed by hand.

## 1. Endorse the customers who sell

Each of your customers that sells by card must be
verified first. This is the customer's `card_checkout` endorsement, next to its
other endorsements. Individual and business customers can both be endorsed.

### Submit the endorsement

```http
POST /v1/card-checkout/customers/{customer_id}/endorsement
```

No body. What the customer already gave Yativo is used straight away:

- **Business customer:** its KYB submission (legal name, entity type, tax and
  registration numbers, registered address, and the signer among its associated
  persons) is sent for verification. The customer then uploads the company
  documents on the onboarding page.
- **Individual customer:** its KYC submission (name, date of birth, address,
  phone, ID document) is sent for verification when its ID photos and a selfie
  are on file. Otherwise the customer confirms the prefilled details and adds
  the photos on the onboarding page.

```json
{
  "status": "success",
  "status_code": 202,
  "message": "Endorsement submitted",
  "data": {
    "customer_id": "4f0c1e1a-…",
    "customer_type": "business",
    "endorsement_status": "pending",
    "verification_status": "pending",
    "can_accept_payments": false,
    "onboarding_url": "https://…/onboarding/…?expires=…&signature=…",
    "onboarding_url_expires_at": "2026-10-16T14:00:00+00:00"
  }
}
```

Send the customer to `onboarding_url`. It opens a Yativo-hosted page showing
only the form for the customer's type, prefilled with what is on file:

- **Individual:** confirm the details, add a government ID and a selfie.
- **Business:** confirm the company details, then upload the company documents
  and the representative's ID in a secure step embedded in the same page.

If the review needs more (a clearer photo, an extra detail), the same page
lists each item and takes the answers and uploads.

You can also submit the endorsement with the customer endpoint you already use
for other endorsements:
`POST /v1/customer/kyc/resubmit/{customer_id}/card_checkout` (see
[KYC endorsement resubmission](/yativo-fiat/kyc#resubmit-one-endorsement); for
`card_checkout` it accepts business customers too).

Submitting again is safe: an approved customer is left as it is, and nothing
already sent is sent twice.

### Before your account is enabled

A customer can ask for card checkout before your account has access. In that
case the endorsement request is held instead of refused:

- If you never requested access, the request is filed for you
  (`access_status` becomes `requested`) and the note says which customer asked.
- The customer's `card_checkout` endorsement is set to `pending`, with
  `metadata.awaiting_business_access: true` and an `errors` entry explaining
  that access was requested. You receive the usual `endorsement.updated` webhook.
- The response is `202`:

```json
{
  "status": "success",
  "status_code": 202,
  "message": "Card checkout access has been requested for this business. This request continues automatically once it is approved.",
  "data": {
    "customer_id": "4f0c1e1a-…",
    "endorsement_status": "pending",
    "awaiting_business_access": true
  }
}
```

When our team enables your account, every waiting customer is submitted
automatically and you get `endorsement.updated` webhooks as they progress. You
don't need to call the endpoint again.

If your access request was **declined**, the endorsement request returns `403`
with `code: card_checkout_access_declined`. Send a new
[access request](#request-access) with more detail first.

### Follow the endorsement

The `card_checkout` endorsement appears with the customer's other endorsements
on the customer object (`GET /v1/customer/{customer_id}`), and
every change sends you the usual `endorsement.updated` webhook. While there is
something for the customer to do, `hosted_kyc_url` holds the onboarding link;
it is renewed automatically whenever you read the endorsements, so the one you
get always works.

You can also read it on its own:

```http
GET /v1/card-checkout/customers/{customer_id}
```

The response has the same shape as the submit response.

| `endorsement_status` | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `not_started` | Not submitted yet. |
| `submitted` | Received; the verification is being prepared. |
| `pending` | Waiting for the customer: send them to `onboarding_url`. `message` says what's missing. |
| `under_review` | Everything was sent; under review, usually within 3 business days. |
| `approved` | The customer can sell. `can_accept_payments` is `true`. |
| `rejected` | Not approved, or the customer's account was suspended. Contact support. |

### A fresh onboarding link

```http
POST /v1/card-checkout/customers/{customer_id}/onboarding
Content-Type: application/json

{ "return_url": "https://app.example.com/done", "refresh_url": "https://app.example.com/link-expired" }
```

Both fields are optional https URLs: `return_url` is the "Back to app" link on
the page, `refresh_url` is where the customer lands once this link has expired.
Returns `{ "customer_id", "endorsement_status", "onboarding_url", "expires_at" }`.
This link is valid for 2 hours.

**2. Submit corrections** (business customers)

```http
POST /v1/card-checkout/customers/{customer_id}/verification
Content-Type: application/json
```

Any field you leave out is taken from `prefill`. A field you send overrides it.

| Field | Type | Required | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| `kind` | string | yes | Always `business`. |
| `business_name` | string | yes, unless prefilled | Legal business name. |
| `country` | ISO 3166-1 alpha-2 | yes, unless prefilled | Country of incorporation, e.g. `US`. |
| `business_structure` | string | no | Legal entity structure, e.g. `private_corporation`. Must be valid for `country`; omit it for countries with no structures. |
| `business_tax_identification_number` | string | no | EIN (US), Business Number (CA), Company Number (UK). |
| `place_of_incorporation` | string | no | State or region of incorporation, e.g. `DE`. |
| `business_website` | URL | no | The business's own site or social page. |
| `address[line1]`, `address[line2]`, `address[city]`, `address[state]`, `address[postal_code]` | string | no | Registered business address. |
| `first_name` | string | yes, unless prefilled | Business representative's first name. |
| `last_name` | string | yes, unless prefilled | Business representative's last name. |
| `date_of_birth` | `YYYY-MM-DD` | no | Representative's date of birth (18 or older). |
| `tax_identification_number` | string | no | Representative's personal tax ID (SSN in the US). Sent once; Yativo does not store it. |

Returns `201`:

```json
{
  "status": "success",
  "status_code": 201,
  "message": "Verification submitted",
  "data": {
    "verification_status": "action_required",
    "information_requested": [
      { "id": "inrqi_…", "requirement": "business_verification_document", "type": "files", "label": "Company Verification Document" },
      { "id": "inrqi_…", "requirement": "identity_document", "type": "id_document", "label": "Identity Document", "options": ["PASSPORT", "DRIVERS"] }
    ]
  }
}
```

| `verification_status` | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `not_started` | Nothing submitted yet. |
| `pending` | Submitted; for a business, the company documents are still to be uploaded on the onboarding page. |
| `processing` | Automated checks running. |
| `action_required` | Answer the items in `information_requested` (step 3). |
| `manual_review` | Under review, usually within 3 business days. Nothing to do. |
| `approved` | Verified. |
| `rejected` | Not approved. Contact support. |

**3. Answer what the review asks for** (any customer)

Each item in `information_requested` is one thing to collect:

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| `id` | Send it back with the answer. |
| `requirement` | What is needed, e.g. `business_verification_document`, `identity_document`, `ein`, `business_phone_number`. |
| `type` | How to answer: `files`, `id_document`, `text`, `text_with_files`, `date`, `phone`, `select` or `address`. |
| `label` | Text to show the user. |
| `options` | Allowed values for a `select` item; accepted ID types for an `id_document` item. |
| `multiple` | `true` when a `select` item takes several values. |
| `optional` | `true` when the item can be skipped. |
| `files_allowed` | `true` when a text answer can carry supporting documents. |
| `errors` | Why a previous answer was rejected. Fix it and answer again. |

```http
POST /v1/card-checkout/customers/{customer_id}/verification/information
Content-Type: multipart/form-data
```

| Item `type` | Send |
|---|---|
| `files` | `uploads[<id>][files][]`: one file per page, in order. If the item has `options`, also `answers[<id>][value]`, the document you are sending. |
| `id_document` | `answers[<id>][document_type]` (one of `options`), the files `uploads[<id>][documents][<slot>]`, and optionally `answers[<id>][issuing_country]` and `answers[<id>][document_number]`. |
| `text`, `date`, `phone` | `answers[<id>][value]`. Dates are `YYYY-MM-DD`; phone numbers include the country code (`+12125550123`). |
| `text_with_files` | `answers[<id>][value]`, plus optional `uploads[<id>][files][]`. |
| `select` | `answers[<id>][value]`, or `answers[<id>][values][]` when `multiple` is `true`. |
| `address` | `answers[<id>][address][line1]`, `[line2]`, `[city]`, `[state]`, `[postal_code]`, `[country]`. No PO boxes. |

ID document slots:

| `document_type` | Slots |
|---|---|
| `PASSPORT` | `passport_front` |
| `ID_CARD` | `id_card_front`, `id_card_back` |
| `DRIVERS` | `drivers_front`, `drivers_back` |
| `RESIDENCE_PERMIT` | `residence_permit_front`, `residence_permit_back` |

Files are JPG, PNG or PDF, up to 10 MB each and 10 per item (a `selfie` must be
JPG or PNG). Yativo forwards them for review and does not keep them.

You can answer some items now and the rest later; answered items leave the
list. Everything is checked before anything is sent, so one bad answer sends
nothing. The response has the same shape as the submit response.

Errors from these endpoints:

| Status | Code | When |
|---|---|---|
| 404 | `customer_not_found` | Not one of your customers. |
| 409 | `endorsement_not_submitted` | Submit the `card_checkout` endorsement first. |
| 409 | `already_verified` | The customer is already verified. |
| 409 | `verification_not_started` | Nothing submitted yet to answer. |
| 409 | `nothing_requested` | Nothing is needed right now. |
| 422 | `use_onboarding_page` | Individual customers add their ID and selfie on the onboarding page. |
| 422 | `missing_fields` | A required detail isn't on file and wasn't sent. |
| 422 | `invalid_business_structure` | `business_structure` isn't valid for `country`. |
| 422 | `verification_refused` | A detail was rejected; the message says which. |
| 422 | — | `errors` keyed `answers.<id>` says what to fix. |
| 502 | `provider_unavailable` | Temporarily unavailable; retry. |

## 2. Create a checkout order

```http
POST /v1/card-checkout/orders
Content-Type: application/json
Idempotency-Key: 3f1c2a1e-8b7d-4c1e-9f0a-6d2b5e7c9a10
```

| Field | Type | Required | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| `customer_id` | string | yes | Your customer that is selling. Its `card_checkout` endorsement must be `approved`. |
| `amount` | string | yes | Amount to charge, e.g. `"49.99"`. |
| `currency` | string | yes | `USD`. |
| `description` | string | yes | What the buyer is paying for (shown on the checkout page, max 255). |
| `reference` | string | no | Your own order id. Unique per account (letters, digits, `._:-/`, max 100). |
| `customer_email` | string | no | The **buyer's** email; pre-fills the checkout form. |
| `customer_name` | string | no | The **buyer's** name; pre-fills the checkout form. |
| `success_url` | https URL | no | "Continue" link shown after a successful payment. |
| `cancel_url` | https URL | no | "Return to seller" link shown on the checkout page. |
| `metadata` | object | no | Up to 20 string values, returned on the order and in webhooks. |
| `payment_methods` | string[] | no | Limit this checkout to some payment methods, e.g. `["card", "apple_pay"]`. Each must be in the `payment_methods` list from [`GET /v1/card-checkout/account`](#your-balance). Leave it out to offer every enabled method. |

```bash
curl -X POST https://api.yativo.com/api/v1/card-checkout/orders \
  -H "X-Api-Key: $YATIVO_API_KEY" \
  -H "X-Api-Secret: $YATIVO_API_SECRET" \
  -H "Idempotency-Key: $(uuidgen)" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
        "customer_id": "4f0c1e1a-…",
        "amount": "49.99",
        "currency": "USD",
        "description": "Pro plan — 1 month",
        "reference": "INV-10023",
        "customer_email": "ada@example.com",
        "success_url": "https://shop.example.com/thanks",
        "cancel_url": "https://shop.example.com/cart",
        "metadata": { "plan": "pro" }
      }'
```

**Response `201`**

```json
{
  "status": "success",
  "status_code": 201,
  "message": "Checkout created",
  "data": {
    "id": "cco_7wq2k9x0m3c8v1b5n6z4l2j8h0g4f6d1",
    "customer_id": "4f0c1e1a-…",
    "reference": "INV-10023",
    "description": "Pro plan — 1 month",
    "status": "pending",
    "currency": "USD",
    "amount": "49.99",
    "platform_fee": "2.05",
    "reserve": "0.00",
    "net_amount": "47.94",
    "refunded_amount": "0.00",
    "disputed_amount": "0.00",
    "checkout_url": "https://pay.yativo.com/card/cco_7wq2…?expires=…&signature=…",
    "metadata": { "plan": "pro" },
    "expires_at": "2026-10-08T15:00:00+00:00",
    "created_at": "2026-10-08T14:00:00+00:00",
    "updated_at": "2026-10-08T14:00:00+00:00"
  }
}
```

- `platform_fee`, `reserve` and `net_amount` are fixed when the order is created.
  Pricing changes made later don't affect existing orders.
- The checkout link expires after 60 minutes (`expires_at`). Create a new order
  if the customer comes back later.
- A `reference` you've already used returns `409` with `code: duplicate_reference`.
- A `payment_methods` entry that isn't enabled returns `422` with
  `code: payment_method_not_enabled`. When you limit the methods, the order
  includes them as `payment_methods`.
- A customer whose endorsement isn't approved returns `403` with
  `code: customer_not_endorsed`; another business's customer returns `404`.

## 3. Send the buyer to the checkout page

Redirect the buyer to `checkout_url` exactly as returned. Don't change it:
the URL is signed, and any edit makes it invalid.

On the checkout page, which shows your customer as the seller, the buyer:

1. enters their email, name and billing address, then their card details;
2. completes 3-D Secure if their bank asks for it;
3. lands on a receipt page showing the confirmed result, with a link to your
   `success_url` if you set one.

The page supports light and dark mode and works on mobile.

> **Buyer activity log.** To protect you and your customers from fraud, the
> checkout page records the buyer's actions on the page, device and browser
> details, IP address and approximate location. The page tells the buyer
> this. The log is used for fraud review and dispute evidence.

## 4. Follow orders with webhooks

Card Checkout events go to the webhook URL you registered (see the
[Webhooks Guide](/yativo-platform/webhooks/overview)), signed in the same way as every other event.
You get one for **every change** in an order's status, and for every refund or
dispute amount change:

| Event | When |
|---|---|
| `card_checkout.order.created` | The order was created. |
| `card_checkout.order.requires_action` | The buyer's bank asked for verification (3-D Secure). |
| `card_checkout.order.processing` | A payment was submitted; waiting for confirmation. |
| `card_checkout.order.paid` | Payment confirmed. **Fulfil the order now.** |
| `card_checkout.order.payment_failed` | An attempt failed. The buyer can still retry on the same link. |
| `card_checkout.order.under_review` | Payment received but held for manual review. |
| `card_checkout.order.partially_refunded`, `card_checkout.order.refunded` | Part or all of the payment was refunded. |
| `card_checkout.order.disputed` | The buyer opened a chargeback. |
| `card_checkout.order.expired` | The link expired before payment. |

The payload is the order, including the `customer_id` that sold it:

```json
{
  "event.type": "card_checkout.order.paid",
  "payload": {
    "id": "cco_7wq2k9x0m3c8v1b5n6z4l2j8h0g4f6d1",
    "customer_id": "4f0c1e1a-…",
    "reference": "INV-10023",
    "description": "Pro plan — 1 month",
    "status": "paid",
    "currency": "USD",
    "amount": "49.99",
    "platform_fee": "2.05",
    "reserve": "0.00",
    "net_amount": "47.94",
    "refunded_amount": "0.00",
    "disputed_amount": "0.00",
    "metadata": { "plan": "pro" },
    "paid_at": "2026-10-08T14:05:00+00:00",
    "created_at": "2026-10-08T14:00:00+00:00",
    "updated_at": "2026-10-08T14:05:00+00:00"
  }
}
```

Make your handler idempotent. You may receive an event more than once, and
events can arrive out of order. When in doubt, read the order with
[`GET /v1/card-checkout/orders/{id}`](#read-orders) and act on its `status`.

On sandbox you can send a sample Card Checkout event to your registered URL
without creating an order: `POST /api/v1/demo/webhook/card_checkout` (defaults to
`card_checkout.order.paid`) or `POST /api/v1/demo/webhook/card_checkout.order.expired`.
See [Testing on sandbox](/yativo-platform/testing/sandbox-testing).

## Your balance

```http
GET /v1/card-checkout/account
```

```json
{
  "status": "success",
  "status_code": 200,
  "message": "Request successful",
  "data": {
    "enabled": true,
    "access_status": "enabled",
    "currencies": ["USD"],
    "payment_methods": ["card", "apple_pay", "google_pay"],
    "pricing": {
      "fee_percent": 3.5,
      "fee_fixed": "0.30",
      "reserve_percent": 0,
      "settlement_delay_days": 0
    },
    "balance": { "currency": "USD", "available": "120.50", "on_hold": "35.00", "total": "155.50" },
    "balance_by_customer": [
      { "customer_id": "4f0c1e1a-…", "available": "100.00", "on_hold": "35.00", "total": "135.00" },
      { "customer_id": "9b2d7c44-…", "available": "20.50", "on_hold": "0.00", "total": "20.50" }
    ]
  }
}
```

`balance` is what Yativo owes you from your customers' card sales:

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `available` | Settled: past the settlement delay and reserve period. Negative if a refund or dispute is owed. |
| `on_hold` | Still in the settlement delay or reserve period. |
| `total` | `available` + `on_hold`. |

`balance_by_customer` splits it by the customer whose sales it came from
(`customer_id` is `null` for amounts not tied to a customer). Use it to settle
with your customers.

The customer's own money stays on their connected account. They can withdraw
**only Yativo `available`** for that customer — funds that have cleared the
business-day hold and any retention. Pending and reserved amounts cannot be
withdrawn.

`GET /v1/card-checkout/customers/{id}` includes `balance` when the endorsement
is `approved`:

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `available` | Cleared on Yativo. This is the maximum they can withdraw. |
| `pending` | Still in the clearing window. Not withdrawable. |
| `reserved` | Retention still held after clearing. Not withdrawable. |
| `on_hold` | `pending` + `reserved`. |
| `total` | `available` + `on_hold`. |
| `next_available_at` | When the next pending amount becomes `available`. |

An amount above `available` returns `422` with `code: amount_exceeds_available`
and the current `balance`. Each customer can only manage their own payout
destinations and can only withdraw to those destinations.

Save bank details for this customer through the API (never stored by Yativo):

```bash
curl -G https://api.yativo.com/api/v1/card-checkout/customers/CUSTOMER_ID/supported-payout-methods \
  -H "X-Api-Key: $YATIVO_API_KEY" \
  -H "X-Api-Secret: $YATIVO_API_SECRET" \
  --data-urlencode "country=US" \
  --data-urlencode "supported_payout_method_id=podst_ach"

curl -X POST https://api.yativo.com/api/v1/card-checkout/customers/CUSTOMER_ID/payout-methods \
  -H "X-Api-Key: $YATIVO_API_KEY" \
  -H "X-Api-Secret: $YATIVO_API_SECRET" \
  -H "Idempotency-Key: 9e2d6a14-5c3b-4f8e-a7d1-0b4c2e6f8a35" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"supported_payout_method_id":"podst_ach","nickname":"Ops checking","fields":{"fld_bank_account_number":"8825310472","fld_bank_routing_number":"111000025","fld_bank_account_type":"checking"}}'
```

The customer must already have an approved `card_checkout` endorsement.
`fields` is keyed by the `fld_…` ids from `GET …/supported-payout-methods`.

After a destination is saved, withdraw cleared funds:

```bash
curl -X POST https://api.yativo.com/api/v1/card-checkout/customers/CUSTOMER_ID/payouts \
  -H "X-Api-Key: $YATIVO_API_KEY" \
  -H "X-Api-Secret: $YATIVO_API_SECRET" \
  -H "Idempotency-Key: withdraw-2026-11-09-1" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"amount":"50.00"}'
```

`payout_method_id` is optional and must already appear on
`GET …/payout-methods` for that customer. List withdrawals with
`GET …/payouts`. Cancel an in-flight one with `POST …/payouts/{id}/cancel`.

## Fees, settlement and reserves

For every paid order:

```
platform_fee = amount × fee_percent + fee_fixed      (capped at the amount)
reserve      = (amount − platform_fee) × reserve_percent
net_amount   = amount − platform_fee − reserve
```

- **Settlement delay.** `net_amount` is added to your balance, against the
  selling customer, as soon as the payment is confirmed, and becomes
  `available` after `settlement_delay_days` (often 0). Until then it counts as
  `on_hold` / `pending` and cannot be withdrawn.
- **Reserve.** If your account has a reserve, that part is `reserved` until
  the retention period ends. It cannot be withdrawn until then.

---

## Refunds and disputes

Refunds and chargebacks come out of your balance, against the customer that
made the sale:

- The refunded or disputed amount is deducted from your balance. If that makes
  the balance negative, your next sales cover it.
- **Disputes:** the disputed amount is deducted when the dispute opens. If you
  win, it is credited back. Pre-dispute inquiries don't move funds.
- The full refunded amount is deducted. The platform fee on a refunded order is
  not returned.
